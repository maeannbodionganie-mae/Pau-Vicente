01 / The person behind the purpose

Becoming the mentor
I once needed.

I started freelancing during the pandemic with no experience and no roadmap, just the willingness to learn.

The road wasn’t easy. I navigated difficult clients, managed multiple accounts, and worked days that sometimes stretched beyond 14 hours. Every mistake became a lesson, and every setback strengthened my resilience.

Today, I strive to be the mentor I once needed, guiding aspiring freelancers toward meaningful careers and helping them discover opportunities beyond the traditional 9-to-5 path.

02 / My story · Four chapters

A leap of faith.
A purpose worth sharing.

From corporate life to freelancing, mentoring, and building opportunities for others.

01The beginning02The leap03The mentor04Cross Core

01Where it began

The corporate
beginning.

Before stepping into freelancing, I worked as a Marketing Assistant for a corporate company in the Philippines. I genuinely enjoyed the work, especially organizing corporate events and collaborating with different teams.

Over time, I began to feel burned out. I poured so much time and energy into my job while feeling that my efforts weren’t reflected in the opportunities and compensation I received. Deep down, I wanted more room to grow, create a greater impact, and have more freedom over my career.

Room to grow.
Freedom to create.

Before the leap,
there was a question:
what else is possible?

02A new direction

The leap into
freelancing.

When the pandemic changed the way we worked, I took a leap of faith and applied as a Virtual Assistant. With no roadmap and very little knowledge of freelancing, the beginning was filled with uncertainty, trial and error, and plenty of learning along the way.

Everything changed when I landed my first client. For the first time, I experienced what it felt like to have my work valued and appreciated. That gave me the confidence to keep going, work with multiple clients, and continue sharpening my skills.

The first client.
A new sense of possibility.

03Passing it forward

Becoming the mentor
I once needed.

What began as simple conversations
grew into something much bigger.

As my freelance career grew, I realized I couldn’t take on every opportunity myself. Around the same time, my closest friends started asking what I did, how freelancing worked, and how they could begin.

I started mentoring them, sharing what I had learned through my own experiences. What began as simple conversations grew into something much bigger: a passion for helping others build meaningful careers.

04Growing together

Building
Cross Core.

That passion became the foundation of Cross Core, the digital marketing agency I now lead.

More than providing digital marketing services, Cross Core was built to create opportunities and equip aspiring freelancers with practical skills. Its mission reflects what I have come to believe: success becomes more meaningful when others grow with you.

Growth means more
when it’s shared.

Creating opportunities.
Equipping people.
Growing alongside them.
